Rating instructions / 评分须知

Judge fulfillment of the request, not music quality or personal preference. Listen to each clip in full before rating; replay is allowed. Rate explicit, inferred, and overall intent independently on a 1–5 scale; tied best candidates are allowed. Item ratings use 1–20, normalized as (r − 1) / 19; uncertain responses are excluded from numerical analysis. Full protocol: manuscript Appendix B and Table 6.

只评价请求满足程度，不评价音乐质量或个人喜好。评分前完整试听，可重复播放。明确、补全和整体意图分别独立打 1–5 分，可选并列最佳。逐项打 1–20 分，按 (r − 1) / 19 归一化；不确定不参与数值分析。完整协议见论文附录 B、表 6。
